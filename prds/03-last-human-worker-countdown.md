# PRD: The Last Human Worker Countdown

**Codename:** Last Worker
**Category:** Public Good / Viral
**Build time:** 2-3 days
**Revenue:** Sponsorship, newsletter funnel

---

## Concept
A countdown timer estimating **when each profession will be majority-automated** — based on current AI capability trajectories.

Not "when will AI replace all doctors" — but "when will >50% of current doctor TASKS be done by AI."

## Visual Design
- Searchable list of 200+ professions
- Each shows: a progress bar (% of tasks automatable today) + countdown timer
- Sort by: soonest, furthest, most change since last month
- Hero section: "The median profession hits 50% automation by [date]"
- Heatmap view: industries colored by automation proximity

## Data Sources
- O*NET task database (BLS) — breakdown of tasks per profession
- AI benchmark scores on those specific tasks
- Research papers on AI capability by domain
- McKinsey/WEF automation estimates (calibrated with actual AI performance)
- Monthly recalculation as new models/tools ship

## Revenue Path
- Free: browse 50 professions
- Premium ($9/mo): all 200+, custom alerts ("notify me when my job hits 60%"), sector reports
- Corporate: $199/mo for HR workforce planning teams
- Sponsorship: "Upskilling partner" slot

## Why This Goes Viral
"When will YOUR job be automated?" — everyone will search for their profession. Inherently shareable. Media catnip (journalists will write about it).

## Kill Criteria
If methodology is credibly challenged in first month with no defense, revise or sunset.
