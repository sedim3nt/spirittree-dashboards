# PRD: The Human Thought Clock

**Codename:** Thought Clock
**Category:** Public Good / Attention Magnet
**Build time:** 1-2 days
**Revenue:** Ad-supported, API access

---

## Concept
A real-time counter showing **how long it takes AI to process everything a human brain thinks in an entire day.**

The average human has ~6,200 thoughts per day, generating roughly 70,000-80,000 internal cognitive events. Each thought averages ~250ms of neural processing. Total human "thought time" per day: ~5.5 hours of active cognition.

GPT-5.4 processes 2M tokens in seconds. Claude 4.6 processes 1M tokens in ~30 seconds.

**The dashboard shows a clock counting up from 00:00:00.000 — and stops when the AI has processed the equivalent of a full day of human cognition.** Current models: it stops around **4-8 seconds.**

## Visual Design
- Giant digital clock in the center, counting milliseconds
- Stops with a dramatic flash when it hits "1 human day"
- Below: comparison bars — "Human: 24 hours | GPT-5.4: 4.2 seconds | Claude 4.6: 7.8 seconds"
- Historical chart showing this number shrinking monthly
- Bottom: "At current pace, AI will process a human lifetime of thought in [X] seconds by [date]"

## Data Sources

- Neuroscience research on human cognition (published papers)
- Updated monthly as new models release
