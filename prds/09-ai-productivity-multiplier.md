# PRD: The AI Productivity Multiplier Calculator

**Codename:** Multiplier
**Category:** Revenue / Lead Gen
**Build time:** 2 days
**Revenue:** Consulting funnel, enterprise

---

## Concept
An interactive calculator that answers: **"How much more productive would YOUR team be with AI?"**

Input your team size, roles, and common tasks. Output: hours saved per week, cost reduction, and ROI timeline.

## How It Works

### Step 1: Input
- Team size (1-1000)
- Roles (dropdown: engineer, designer, marketer, analyst, writer, ops, customer service, etc.)
- Hours per week on: email, writing, research, coding, data analysis, meetings, admin

### Step 2: Calculation
Each task has an **AI multiplier** based on current model capabilities:
| Task | AI Multiplier | Source |
|------|--------------|--------|
| Email drafting | 4x | Internal benchmarks |
| Code generation | 3-5x | GitHub Copilot research |
| Research/analysis | 5-8x | McKinsey 2025 report |
| Content writing | 3-6x | Various studies |
| Data entry | 10-20x | Automation benchmarks |
| Customer support | 4-7x | Intercom/Zendesk data |
| Meeting summaries | 10x | Otter.ai benchmarks |

### Step 3: Output
- **Hours saved per week:** [X] hours (equivalent of [Y] full-time employees)
- **Annual cost savings:** $[Z] (at average loaded cost)
- **ROI timeline:** "AI tools pay for themselves in [N] weeks"
- **Comparison:** "Your team of 10 with AI = a team of [X] without"
- Downloadable PDF report

## Visual Design

## Why This Works
ROI calculators are the highest-converting content format in B2B SaaS. Every company is asking "should we invest in AI?" This gives them a number. And positions us as the experts who can implement it.
